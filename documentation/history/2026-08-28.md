Periodic Review / Troubleshooting scalars, and the support block. The
Periodic Notes sync mode became a `visible:`-gated banner plus `disabled:`
predicates on every period control, with `setControlValue` rejecting synced
writes as the real guarantee. `updateSettings` is the serialized
persist-then-commit write path; `applyDebugLogging` and
`notifySettingsChanged` fire strictly after the commit, and the sync-state
listeners re-render via `update()` (not the never-called `display()`).
